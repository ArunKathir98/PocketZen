16.1 Known limitations (be honest with users)

  • Reminders alert only while the app can run; there is no alert for a fully closed or locked phone (needs a push server).
  • Notification sound cannot be customised from a website.
  • Without a Google account, data lives only on that device: clearing browser data or losing the phone loses it unless backed up.
  • Group editing is admin-only; group view links are readable by anyone who has them until the admin deletes the group.
  • Branding on Google's consent screen needs a domain you own.
  • The PIN is a casual privacy lock, not strong security.
  • Interest uses a simple month/day calendar approximation; confirm against a lender's statement for legal disputes.
