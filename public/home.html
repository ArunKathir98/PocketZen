PocketZen

Your Money. Your Control.

PocketZen is a personal finance companion to track income, manage expenses, organize accounts, set budgets, keep tabs on EMIs and loans, split bills with friends, and stay on top of reminders — with your financial records stored in your own Google Drive.

  • Record income, expenses and transfers between accounts
  • Organize categories and accounts (savings, wallet, cash, investment) and see every transaction in a category
  • Set monthly budgets and see what's left
  • Track EMIs: total, months, pending months and payments
  • Money lent or borrowed, with fixed, simple or compound interest and shareable statements
  • Group expenses: you manage a group, friends open a view-only link, and PocketZen suggests who pays whom (with a PDF summary)
  • Bill reminders and a daily expense nudge, with sound and vibration
  • Optional PIN, light and dark mode, password-protected backups, export and import
  • Installable on phone, tablet and desktop, and it works offline

PocketZen signs you in with Google and saves your data in a private, hidden app folder in your Drive that only PocketZen can access. There is no PocketZen server or database: shared groups are files in the group admin's own Drive, readable by link. Prefer not to use a Google account? Use the simple version (Records, Categories, Accounts) with data stored only on your device, plus backup files, and connect Google later.
