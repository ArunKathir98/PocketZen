PocketZen

Your Money. Your Control.

PocketZen is a personal finance companion to track income, manage expenses, organize accounts, set budgets, and stay on top of bills — with your financial records stored in your own Google Drive.

  • Record income, expenses and transfers between accounts
  • Organize categories and accounts (savings, wallet, cash, investment)
  • Set monthly budgets and see what's left
  • Bill reminders that repeat daily, weekly or monthly
  • Optional app PIN, light and dark mode, export and import
  • Installable on phone, tablet and desktop

PocketZen signs you in with Google and saves your data in a private, hidden app folder in your Drive that only PocketZen can access. There is no PocketZen server or database. Prefer not to use a Google account? You can also use the app with data stored only on your device, with backup files.
