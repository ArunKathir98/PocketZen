Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

PocketZen ("the app") is a personal finance tracker. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes. In short: your financial data stays in your own Google Drive and on your own device. We do not collect, receive, store or sell it.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account basics (name, email address, profile picture) — read when you sign in, only to show who is signed in and to keep your session on your device.
  • Google Drive app data folder (drive.appdata scope) — the app creates and reads a single file, pocketzen-data.json, in a hidden folder that only PocketZen can access. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Financial records you enter — accounts, categories, transactions, budgets, reminders and settings. They are saved in that Drive file and cached in your browser's local storage so the app works offline.
  • App PIN (optional) — stored only as a salted, hashed value; the PIN itself is never stored or sent anywhere.

How information is used

Data is used only to provide the app's features for you: showing your records, calculating totals and budgets, syncing between your devices, and sending local reminder notifications. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling, and is not shared with third parties.

Using PocketZen without an account

You can choose Continue without an account. In that case no Google sign-in happens and your data is saved only in your browser's storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or to Google. You can create password-protected backup files, and you can connect Google Drive later, at which point your on-device data is copied to your own Drive. If you use PocketZen this way, clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes your records unless you have a backup.

Where data is stored

Your data lives in your Google Drive (if you sign in with Google) and in your browser's storage on your own device. The developer operates no server or database that receives your data. The app's code is hosted as a static website.

Google API Services disclosure

PocketZen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

  • Log out (or Erase data if you use the app without an account) in the app to remove the session and locally stored data from that device.
  • Export your data at any time from the Profile page.
  • Delete your data by removing PocketZen's app data in Google Drive (Drive → Settings → Manage apps → PocketZen → Delete hidden app data), and/or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
