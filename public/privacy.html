Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

PocketZen ("the app") is a personal finance tracker. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes. In short: your financial data stays in your own Google Drive and on your own device. We do not run a server that collects, receives, stores or sells it.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account basics (name, email address, profile picture) — read when you sign in, only to show who is signed in and to keep your session on your device.
  • Google Drive app data folder (drive.appdata permission) — the app creates and reads a single file, pocketzen-data.json, in a hidden folder that only PocketZen can access. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Files PocketZen creates for shared groups (drive.file permission) — requested only if you create a group. The app can then create, update and delete only the group files it created. It cannot see your other files.
  • Financial records you enter — accounts, categories, transactions, budgets, EMIs, loans, reminders, group membership and settings. They are saved in the app data file and cached on your device so the app works offline.
  • App PIN (optional) — stored only as a salted, hashed value; the PIN itself is never stored or sent anywhere.

How information is used

Data is used only to provide the app's features for you: showing your records, calculating totals, budgets, loan interest and group balances, syncing between your devices, and sending local reminder notifications. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling, and is not shared with third parties.

Shared groups (optional)

A group is saved as a file in the admin's own Google Drive, and the app makes it readable by anyone who has its link. People with the link can view the group name, the names (and any email addresses) the admin entered, and the expenses and payments, without signing in. Only the admin can change or delete it. Share the link only with people you trust, and avoid putting sensitive information in a group. The admin can stop sharing at any time by deleting the group in the app (or the file in Drive). PocketZen runs no server for groups: viewers' devices read the file directly from Google using a public API key.

Using PocketZen without an account

You can choose Continue without an account. In that case no Google sign-in happens and your data is saved only in your browser's storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to us or to Google. You can create password-protected backup files, and you can connect Google Drive later, at which point your on-device data is copied to your own Drive. If you use PocketZen this way, clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes your records unless you have a backup.

Where data is stored

Your data lives in your Google Drive (if you sign in with Google) and in your browser's storage on your own device. The developer operates no server or database that receives your data. The app's code is hosted as a static website on Cloudflare.

Google API Services disclosure

PocketZen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deletion

  • Log out (or Erase data if you use the app without an account) in the app to remove the session and locally stored data from that device.
  • Back up your data at any time from the Profile page.
  • Delete a group from inside it, which removes its file from your Drive.
  • Delete your data by removing PocketZen's app data in Google Drive (Drive → Settings → Manage apps → PocketZen → Delete hidden app data), deleting any "PocketZen group" files, and/or revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
